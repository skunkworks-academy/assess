# How to use this portal

Each personal page is a working document. It provides a four-week experiment, prompts for reflection, and a practical evidence checklist. Adapt it with your assessment findings and a conversation with your mentor or manager.

## 1. Open your roadmap

Go to **Individual roadmaps** in the navigation. Read the suggested starting theme, then decide whether it is the right focus for this month. If not, change it. The content is deliberately editable.

## 2. Make the goal usable

Avoid goals such as “be more confident” or “learn leadership.” Translate them into a behaviour you can repeat and an outcome someone else can see.

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Improve communication | Deliver a two-minute update with a clear ask at the weekly team meeting. |
| Learn data analysis | Clean and explain one real dataset in a one-page insight note. |
| Become a better leader | Run one planning conversation using agenda, decisions, and actions. |

## 3. Book a 20-minute kickoff

Use this agenda with a mentor, manager, or peer:

1. Here is the capability I want to grow.
2. Here is the outcome I will produce in 30 days.
3. Here is the feedback I need from you.
4. What would make this plan more realistic or more useful?

## 4. Run a weekly review

Set aside 15 minutes at the same time each week. Write three short answers:

- What did I practise or produce?
- What feedback or signal did I receive?
- What is the single adjustment for next week?

## 5. Protect your privacy

This site is a shared learning portal, not a place for sensitive assessment responses, confidential customer information, passwords, or personal identifiers. Keep private assessment results in the organisation-approved system and add only the learning actions you are comfortable sharing here.

## 6. Close the loop

At the end of 30 days, save a small evidence pack: your best output, one piece of feedback, and a 150-word reflection. Bring it to your next developmental conversation and choose the next experiment.
