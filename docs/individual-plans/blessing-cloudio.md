# Blessing Cloudio’s Individual Development Roadmap

## Suggested starting theme: data-informed decision making

Validate this focus with your own assessment and work context. The aim is to move from raw information to a decision-ready insight.

### 30-day outcome

> I will improve data-informed decision making by analysing one small dataset or operational information source and communicating three useful insights. I will know it is working when a stakeholder can use the insight to choose a next action.

| Week | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Choose a question that a small dataset can help answer. | Question and data source note. |
| 2 | Clean, organise, and check the information. | Documented data assumptions. |
| 3 | Find patterns and create one clear visual or summary. | Chart, table, or insight note. |
| 4 | Explain the recommendation and invite challenge. | Decision-ready one-pager. |

## Weekly reflection

- What decision could this information improve?
- What does the data not show?
- Can a non-specialist understand the main point in 30 seconds?

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] A decision question
- [ ] A documented data source
- [ ] One clear visual or summary
- [ ] Three evidence-based insights
- [ ] A recommendation and reviewer feedback
