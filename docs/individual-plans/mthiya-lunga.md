# Mthiya Lunga’s Individual Development Roadmap

## Suggested starting theme: secure and reliable ways of working

Validate this theme against your own goals. The focus is on recognising risk, using a repeatable safeguard, and explaining why it matters to others.

### 30-day outcome

> I will improve secure and reliable ways of working by reviewing one workflow for avoidable risk and implementing one practical safeguard. I will know it is working when another person can follow the safer process with confidence.

| Week | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Map one workflow and identify data, access, or handoff risks. | Simple risk map. |
| 2 | Research one relevant control or good practice. | Short explanation in plain language. |
| 3 | Add a lightweight safeguard or checklist. | Draft process update. |
| 4 | Ask a peer to use it and improve the instructions. | Test feedback and revised guide. |

## Weekly reflection

- What could go wrong, and who would be affected?
- Is the safeguard proportionate and easy to use?
- Can I explain the reason for the step without jargon?

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] A workflow or risk map
- [ ] A concise risk explanation
- [ ] A practical safeguard
- [ ] Peer test or review
- [ ] A revised instruction or checklist
