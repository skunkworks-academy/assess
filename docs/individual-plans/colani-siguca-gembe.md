# Colani Siguca Gembe’s Individual Development Roadmap

## Suggested starting theme: value creation and initiative

Validate this with your own aspiration and context. The focus is on finding a real need, testing a useful response, and learning from the result.

### 30-day outcome

> I will improve value creation and initiative by identifying one unmet need and testing a small response with a real audience. I will know it is working when I have evidence of what the audience values and what to improve next.

| Week | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Describe a specific audience, need, and current workaround. | One-page opportunity note. |
| 2 | Create a simple value proposition and test it in conversation. | Feedback notes from two people. |
| 3 | Produce a small demonstration, offer, or experiment. | Prototype, outline, or pilot plan. |
| 4 | Review signals, learning, and the next iteration. | Experiment summary. |

## Weekly reflection

- What job is the audience trying to get done?
- What evidence would show this need is important?
- What did I learn from a response that surprised me?

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Audience and need defined
- [ ] Value proposition
- [ ] Feedback from an intended audience
- [ ] Small test or prototype
- [ ] A decision to continue, change, or stop
