# Fernanda Koppe Souto Maior Macias’s Individual Development Roadmap

## Suggested starting theme: dependable delivery

Validate this theme with your mentor. The focus is on turning a goal into visible milestones, risks, and follow-through.

### 30-day outcome

> I will improve dependable delivery by planning and completing one small piece of work with milestones, owners, risks, and a review. I will know it is working when the work is completed or reprioritised transparently.

| Week | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Define the outcome, scope, stakeholders, and “done” criteria. | One-page delivery plan. |
| 2 | Break work into small tasks and make progress visible. | Updated task board. |
| 3 | Identify one risk early and communicate an option. | Risk note and response. |
| 4 | Review the outcome with a stakeholder and record learning. | Delivery review. |

## Weekly reflection

- Is the next action small enough to begin now?
- Which risk or dependency is not yet visible?
- What would a stakeholder need to know before the next check-in?

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] A definition of done
- [ ] Milestones and owners
- [ ] A risk or dependency log
- [ ] A stakeholder update
- [ ] A short retrospective
