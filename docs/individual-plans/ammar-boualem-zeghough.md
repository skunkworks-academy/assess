# Ammar Boualem Zeghough’s Individual Development Roadmap

## Suggested starting theme: cloud-enabled technical problem solving

Validate this starting theme against your own goals. The focus is on understanding a technical requirement, building a small solution, and documenting it so another person can use or review it.

### 30-day outcome

> I will improve cloud-enabled technical problem solving by designing, building, or documenting one small cloud-related solution to a real or simulated need. I will know it is working when another person can understand the architecture, purpose, and next improvement.

| Week | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Select a clear use case and identify its users, constraints, and success measure. | Use-case brief. |
| 2 | Learn the minimum concepts needed and sketch the architecture. | Annotated diagram. |
| 3 | Build or simulate the smallest useful component. | Demo, configuration notes, or prototype. |
| 4 | Explain the design, trade-offs, and safe next iteration. | Technical walkthrough and feedback. |

## Weekly reflection

- What user or operational outcome does this technical choice support?
- What assumption should be tested before adding complexity?
- Could someone else reproduce or review the work from my notes?

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Use-case brief
- [ ] Architecture or workflow diagram
- [ ] Small working artefact or simulation
- [ ] Notes on trade-offs and safety
- [ ] Peer walkthrough feedback
