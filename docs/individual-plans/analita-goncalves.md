# Analita Gonçalves’s Individual Development Roadmap

## Suggested starting theme: clear communication for action

Validate this theme against your own goals and feedback. The focus is on making messages concise, audience-aware, and easy to act on.

### 30-day outcome

> I will improve action-oriented communication by creating one concise written or spoken update each week. I will know it is working when the audience can identify the context, decision, owner, and next action without clarification.

| Week | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rewrite a long update using context, decision, action, and deadline. | Before-and-after version. |
| 2 | Prepare a two-minute spoken update for a specific audience. | Script or recording notes. |
| 3 | Ask a peer what they understood and what was missing. | Feedback summary. |
| 4 | Deliver a final update and refine a personal communication checklist. | Final output and checklist. |

## Weekly reflection

- Who is the audience and what do they need to decide or do?
- What can be removed without losing meaning?
- Did I make ownership and timing explicit?

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] One clear outcome statement
- [ ] A concise update or presentation
- [ ] Audience feedback
- [ ] A reusable message template
- [ ] A reflection on what changed
