# Christine du Plessis’s Individual Development Roadmap

## Suggested starting theme: collaborative leadership

Validate this theme in your developmental conversation. The focus is on creating clarity and participation so a group can make progress together.

### 30-day outcome

> I will improve collaborative leadership by facilitating one focused planning or problem-solving session with a clear purpose, shared input, decisions, and follow-up. I will know it is working when participants understand what they own next.

| Week | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Select a real group outcome and prepare a simple agenda. | Purpose, agenda, and desired decisions. |
| 2 | Invite input using open questions and active listening. | Notes showing participant contributions. |
| 3 | Make choices, owners, and next steps visible. | Decision and action record. |
| 4 | Ask participants what helped and improve the approach. | Feedback plus facilitation reflection. |

## Weekly reflection

- Whose perspective is missing from this conversation?
- What decision needs to be made before the meeting ends?
- Did I create space for people to contribute and disagree safely?

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Purpose and agenda
- [ ] Notes showing shared input
- [ ] Decisions, owners, and dates
- [ ] Participant feedback
- [ ] One facilitation improvement for next time
